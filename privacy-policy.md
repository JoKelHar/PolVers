This policy covers the PolVers waitlist, website, and — once launched — the PolVers app and its features (posting ideas, voting, the Forum, reports, and provenance certificates). Where a feature is not yet live, the relevant processing begins only when that feature launches.

**During the current waitlist phase, we collect only your email address and your consent choice** (§3.1). The remaining sections describe processing that applies once you create an account and use the app.

## 3. What we collect, and why

### 3.1 Waitlist (now)
- **Your email address**, when you join the waitlist.
- **Purpose:** to send you a single notification when PolVers opens, plus occasional launch updates if you agreed to them.
- **Legal basis:** your consent.

### 3.2 Account and identity (at app launch)
- **Account details:** email, and if you sign in with LinkedIn, the name, profile photo, and email LinkedIn shares with us.
- **Self-declared field(s) of expertise** (e.g. marketing, hardware, finance), which we use to weight your input in your areas.
- **Purpose:** to create and secure your account, and to establish expertise for the Verdict.
- **Legal basis:** performance of our contract with you (providing the service).
- **Note:** LinkedIn sign-in confirms you hold a LinkedIn account; it does not, by itself, verify your identity or your claims of expertise.

### 3.3 The content you create
- **Ideas** you post (headline, body, area, and whether local or universal).
- **Votes and ratings** you cast, and any reasons or commitments you add.
- **Forum contributions** (comments, prior-art flags, offers to build).
- **Provenance records:** a timestamp and a cryptographic hash of each idea you post.
- **Purpose:** to run the arena — display ideas, compute the Roar and the Verdict, power the Forum, and let you prove authorship and date.
- **Legal basis:** performance of our contract with you.

### 3.4 Consent choices
- **Your consent settings** and the time you set them (see §5).
- **Purpose:** to respect your choices and to demonstrate, if asked, that we had a lawful basis to process your data.
- **Legal basis:** our legal obligation, and your consent for the choices themselves.

### 3.5 Payments
- If you buy entries, a subscription, or a provenance certificate, our payment processor handles your card details. **We do not store full card numbers.**
- **Purpose:** to take payment.
- **Legal basis:** performance of our contract with you.

### 3.6 Technical and usage data
- Device, browser, approximate region, and how you use the service, plus security and anti-abuse logs.
- **Purpose:** to keep the service working, safe, and free from fraud and manipulation.
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interests in a secure, functioning service.

## 4. Anonymous mode

You may post an idea in **anonymous mode**, in which your identity is hidden from other users. We still hold the link between you and your idea internally (for security, moderation, and so we can honour your rights), but it is not shown publicly, and anonymous ideas appear in any aggregate statistics without your name.

## 5. Your consent choices (the choices are separate, and you control them)

We never bundle consents. Each of the following is a **separate, opt-in choice, off by default**, and you can turn any of them on or off at any time in your settings:

- **Launch and product emails** — whether we may email you.
- **"Let builders and scouts contact me"** — whether other users may reach you about your ideas.
- **"Include my idea and contact details in reports we sell to organisations"** — whether your details may appear, by name, in paid reports.

If you do not switch on the reports choice, your ideas may still contribute to **anonymous, aggregate** statistics, but your name and contact details are never included in a report. Withdrawing a consent does not affect processing we already carried out lawfully before you withdrew it.

- **Service providers ("processors")** who act on our instructions under a data processing agreement — for example [EMAIL / LIST PROVIDER], [HOSTING PROVIDER], [PAYMENT PROVIDER], and [MODERATION / ANALYTICS PROVIDER, if any]. We choose EU/EEA-hosted providers where we can.

## 10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to: **access** your data; **correct** it; **erase** it; **restrict** or **object** to processing; receive it in a **portable** format; and **withdraw consent** at any time. You also have the right to **lodge a complaint** with a supervisory authority (§13).

To exercise any of these, contact us at [privacy@polvers.com]. We will respond within one month. Using these rights is free, and we will not treat you differently for exercising them.

## 11. Provenance certificates — what they are and aren't

A provenance certificate proves that a specific idea existed under your account at a specific time. It is **evidence of authorship and date only**. It is **not** a patent, a trademark, or any form of ownership of the idea, and it does not stop others from pursuing similar ideas. Please rely on it only for what it is.

## 12. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, and we design our systems so that a purchased seat, a bot, or a coordinated group cannot buy or fake influence over an idea's grade. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep yours safe and will notify you and the authorities of a qualifying breach as the law requires.
